# Warranty Policy

All electronics products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or unauthorized repairs performed by a third party.

To make a warranty claim, customers must provide proof of purchase and a description of the issue. Approved claims result in a repair, replacement, or store credit at the company's discretion.

Extended warranty plans can be purchased separately at checkout for up to 3 additional years of coverage.
