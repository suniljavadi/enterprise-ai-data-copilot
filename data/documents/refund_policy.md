# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging.

Damaged or defective products are eligible for a full refund or replacement at no additional cost, regardless of the 30-day window, as long as the customer reports the issue within 7 days of noticing the damage.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected by our warehouse team.

Sale items and clearance products are final sale and are not eligible for refunds unless the item arrives damaged or defective.
