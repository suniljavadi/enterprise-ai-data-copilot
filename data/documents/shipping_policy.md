# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping typically takes 4-6 business days depending on the destination and carrier.

Express shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee and typically delivers within 1-2 business days.

Orders are shipped from our regional warehouses closest to the customer's shipping address to minimize delivery time.

Customers receive a tracking number by email as soon as their order ships. Delivery delays should be reported to support if the order has not arrived within 3 days of the expected delivery date.
